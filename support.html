Support

Stuck in Ducktown? Email cameron.t.luck@gmail.com and say which device you play on. You’ll hear back from one of the four friends who made the game.

I bought something and didn’t get it

Open the Store, go to the Candy tab and tap Restore Purchases. That brings back the Starter Pack, the VIP Pass and Remove Ads on a new or reset device. Candy packs are used up when you get them, so they can’t be restored; if one didn’t arrive, email us with the date and we’ll sort it out.

Refunds

Refunds go through the store you paid: Apple’s Report a Problem on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play’s refund page on Android.

My progress is gone

Your progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the game or moving to a new phone starts you over. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases.

Ads

Ads between runs stop for good with Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or the VIP Pass. Ads you choose for a prize are always optional. More in the privacy policy.
