Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

DayBreak is a game for iPhone, iPad and Android. This page explains what the game collects and why. In short: you don’t make an account, your progress stays on your device, and the only outside services that see anything about you are the ad network and the store you buy from.

Your progress

Your candy, upgrades, outfits, trophies and settings are saved on your device. We don’t run servers that store them, and we can’t see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, if you’re signed in to Apple’s Game Center, your best Survival distance and your Daily Challenge scores are sent to Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center name. That’s handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in your device’s Settings.

Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob, in two ways:

  • Ads you choose to watch for a prize: getting back up, doubling your candy, the daily treat bag, or a run in a locked art style.
  • An ad between runs: a full-screen ad on the way out of the results screen, at most once every few runs and never in your first few.

To show an ad and count that it was seen, AdMob receives information such as your device type, your IP address, and an advertising identifier.

  • On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks whether DayBreak may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you say no, AdMob doesn’t get your device’s advertising identifier, and the ads you see are less personalised.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the EU), Google’s consent form asks for your choices before any ad loads.
  • How Google uses this information is described in Google’s policy for partner apps.

Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and the VIP Pass turn off the ads between runs. The VIP Pass also gives every ad prize without an ad.

Purchases

In-app purchases go through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We’re told what you bought so the game can hand it over, but we never see your payment details. Restore Purchases asks the store which once-only purchases you own.

Children

DayBreak isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
